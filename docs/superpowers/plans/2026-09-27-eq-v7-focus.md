### T2 — solo in the daemon (worktree v7-t2)
Two-way meter socket: MeterServer reads newline-delimited JSON from each client (buffered, max line 1 KiB, malformed → ignore + log once); `{"solo":{"low":L,"high":H}}` / `{"solo":null}`; owner = that client fd; last writer wins; owner disconnect clears. Processor: `EQProcessor.setSolo(low:high:)` builds a snapshot with two extra HP (2× 2nd-order Butterworth, Q 0.7071) at `low` and two LP at `high` appended after the chain, not saved to config, re-applied on every `applyProfile`/rebuild while active, cleared on engine stop. `MeterFrame.solo {low,high}|null`. Clamp low ≥ 20, high ≤ 0.45·fs, low < high else ignore. Tests: server applies/clears solo on connect/disconnect; processor impulse with solo 300–2800 attenuates 100 Hz and 8 kHz sines by ≥ 20 dB and passes 1 kHz within 1 dB; frame carries solo.
Commit `Solo a frequency range from a meter client`.

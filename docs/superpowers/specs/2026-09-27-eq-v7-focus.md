# eq v7 — focus on an instrument: see it, tune it, hear it alone

Date: 2026-09-27. Status: requested in chat. Builds on v4 zones.

## What the user wants

Zones were a static list under the bars. Wanted: zones **visible live together with the
meter**, and the ability to **pick an instrument so the EQ works only for it** — its range(s):
fundamentals, formants, harmonics.

## Zones become instruments with real ranges (Hz, not only bands)

Each instrument has one or more named ranges; the watch draws them on a frequency axis, not
snapped to band columns.

| Instrument | Ranges (Hz) |
| --- | --- |
| kick | thump 50–100 · beater click 2 000–5 000 |
| bass | fundamental 40–250 · growl/attack 700–1 200 |
| snare | body 150–250 · crack 4 000–6 000 |
| guitar | body 80–1 200 · bite 2 000–5 000 |
| piano | 27–4 200 · brightness 4 000–8 000 |
| voice | fundamental 85–255 · formant F1 300–1 000 · F2 900–2 800 · presence 2 000–5 000 · sibilance 5 000–9 000 |
| cymbals | 6 000–16 000 |
| air | 10 000–20 000 |

Sources are the usual mixing charts (e.g. the Independent Recording Network / iZotope
frequency charts); coarse on purpose, documented as guidance.

## In `eq watch`

- **Zones overlay live.** `z` turns on a zone strip *inside* the meter area: a thin band of
  rows directly above the live row, one row per visible instrument, spans drawn on the same
  log-frequency x-axis as the bars (a band column covers the octave around its centre), range
  names in dim, sub-ranges of one instrument separated by a gap. The strip takes rows from
  the meter (meter ≥ 4 rows kept).
- **Focus.** `[` / `]` (and `Tab`) select an instrument; `Esc` clears. With a focus:
  - header shows `focus: voice (85 Hz–9 kHz)`;
  - bars of bands outside the instrument's ranges turn dim; inside keep their colours; the
    instrument's ranges are drawn as a bracket row `┌─ F1 ─┐ ┌── F2 ──┐` above the bars;
  - digit keys still address all ten bands, but bands outside the focus are refused with a
    footer note (`outside voice — Esc to unfocus`), so tuning stays on the instrument;
  - the live row numbers of focused bands are bright.
- **Listen alone (solo).** `l` toggles solo while focused: the daemon adds a temporary
  high-pass at the instrument's lowest edge and a low-pass at its highest edge (4th-order,
  i.e. two biquads each), so you hear only that instrument's region; multi-range instruments
  solo their outer span. Header shows `SOLO` in yellow. Solo is **never saved**: it lives in
  the daemon only while the watch client that asked for it stays connected, and turns off by
  itself on `l`, `Esc`, quitting, a crash or a dropped socket.

## Protocol

The meter socket becomes two-way. A client may send one JSON line
`{"solo":{"low":300,"high":2800}}` or `{"solo":null}`. The daemon applies it on its main
queue by adding/removing a transient filter pair in the processor snapshot (no config write,
no backup) and clears it when that client disconnects. At most one solo at a time (the last
client wins; its disconnect clears only its own solo).

`eq stream` frames gain `"solo": {"low","high"} | null`.

## `eq zones`

Shows the instrument table with ranges in Hz and the band columns they touch; `--json` gains
`ranges: [{name, low, high}]`.

## Not in scope

Automatic instrument detection, source separation, per-instrument EQ curves (the EQ stays one
curve per device; focus only restricts which bands you edit and what you hear).

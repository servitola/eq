# eq Round F — dynamics and colour, a curve per app

Date: 2026-09-28. Status: approved in chat ("да, давай"). Per-app *mixing* (a separate tap and
curve for every app at once, as SoundSource does) is explicitly out of scope for now.

## F1 — light compression and colour

Both are small and cost nothing when off. Measured after the merge, they added 66 KB to the
stripped release binary. The chain order is EQ → compressor → colour → the existing limiter.

**Compressor** (`eq comp gentle|night|off [--device DEVICE]`):
- Feed-forward and stereo-linked. The detector hears through ITU-R BS.1770 K-weighting
  (a high-pass at 38 Hz, +4 dB above 1.7 kHz), so the level it reads is the loudness a meter
  reads, bass included.
- Gain is smoothed in dB with attack and release coefficients and a soft knee.
- Makeup gain is automatic. `gentle` gives back its reduction averaged over 3 s (up to 6 dB,
  paused in silence), so it changes dynamics and not loudness; `night` adds a fixed makeup that
  restores film dialogue at −24 dBFS RMS, so quiet dialogue comes up.

| Mode | Ratio | Threshold | Knee | Attack | Release | Detector | Makeup | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `gentle` | 2:1 | −18 dBFS | 6 dB | 30 ms | 250 ms | K-weighted RMS, 50 ms | follows the reduction over 3 s | glue |
| `night` | 4:1 | −30 dBFS | 10 dB | 5 ms | 400 ms | K-weighted RMS, 2.5 ms | +4.5 dB fixed | levelling: quiet dialogue up, explosions down |

**Tonal balance (fixed after release).** The first version heard through a 100 Hz high-pass
(24 dB/oct), averaged over 2.5 ms, and gave `gentle` a fixed +3 dB. A user on the `favourite`
curve (+4…+5 dB below 125 Hz) heard "too much bass". `CompressorBalanceTests` runs program
material through the curve with the compressor on and off and compares BS.1770 integrated
loudness and octave-band energy. The fixed makeup was the main cause: the detector read the
bass-boosted mix as quiet, took little, and the makeup added level on top. The long-term tilt
was small on test material; on one real recording with bass-heavy verses and a brighter loud
section it reached +1.5 dB before the limiter, and no detector tried, a flat one included, brought it below +1.15 dB,
because compressing a loud, bright section lowers its treble. Gentle, `favourite` curve, 8 s
sections (tilt = 32–125 Hz against 0.5–2 kHz; pumping = the reduction's swing within each beat):

| Design | Music −14 LUFS: ΔLU / tilt / band spread / reduction on loud parts / pumping | Music −20: ΔLU / reduction | Pink noise: ΔLU | 55 Hz kick alone: swing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| before: HPF 100 Hz, 2.5 ms, +3 dB | +1.07 / −0.39 / 0.52 / 1.47 / 1.68 dB | +2.72 / 0.24 dB | +2.07 | 1.28 dB |
| (a) K-weighting | −0.64 / −0.14 / 0.50 / 3.70 / 2.58 dB | +1.60 / 1.30 dB | +1.05 | 2.95 dB |
| (b) HPF, 50 ms | +1.20 / −0.39 / 0.58 / 1.22 / 1.13 dB | +2.98 / 0.03 dB | +2.25 | 0.36 dB |
| (c) HPF, makeup follows 3 s | −0.15 / −0.07 / 0.25 / 1.47 / 1.68 dB | −0.07 / 0.24 dB | −0.37 | 1.28 dB |
| (bc) HPF, 50 ms, follows 3 s | −0.13 / +0.04 / 0.17 / 1.22 / 1.13 dB | −0.00 / 0.03 dB | −0.30 | 0.36 dB |
| (ab) K, 50 ms, +3 dB | −0.51 / +0.01 / 0.37 / 3.61 / 1.92 dB | +2.15 / 0.94 dB | +1.25 | 2.33 dB |
| (abc) K, 25 ms, follows 3 s | −0.32 / −0.17 / 0.46 / 3.65 / 2.28 dB | −0.08 / 1.07 dB | −0.65 | 2.71 dB |
| **(abc) K, 50 ms, follows 3 s** | −0.27 / −0.08 / 0.34 / 3.61 / 1.92 dB | −0.03 / 0.94 dB | −0.65 | 2.33 dB |

The HPF designs with a following makeup are the most neutral, but they barely compress: 1.2 dB
on loud parts at −14 LUFS and nothing at −20, and a curve's bass boost never reaches the
detector. The chosen design compresses 3.6 dB on loud parts, keeps loudness within 0.7 LU and
every octave band within 0.4 dB of the others, and its pumping per beat is 0.2 dB above the old
design's. On four of the user's own recordings at −14 LUFS (`EQ_EXPLORE_FILES`, not in the repo) it moves loudness by −0.1…−0.7 LU (before:
−0.5…+1.4) and the tilt by −0.3…+0.5 dB (before: −0.3…+0.4). A 1.5 s follow was 0.1–0.2 dB
more neutral and levels whole sections less; 3 s was kept so the makeup does not audibly drift
after every change. A steady 40 Hz tone at −10 dBFS moves the gain by 0.001 dB (`gentle`) and
0.06 dB (`night`) over each cycle.

`night` keeps 2.5 ms and its fixed makeup. On film-like material at −24 LUFS (dialogue at
150 Hz–4 kHz, explosions of 30–200 Hz noise), K-weighting brings explosions over dialogue from
10.0 dB to 6.3 dB (the 100 Hz high-pass: 8.4 dB) and their peaks from −2.5 to −5.6 dBFS (the high-pass
raised them to −1.6), and lifts quiet dialogue from 11.8 to 5.1 dB under loud. A 10 or 25 ms
window levels the same but lets the explosions' peaks through 2–3 dB higher.

**Colour** (`eq color tape|tube <amount 0…1> | off [--device DEVICE]`):
- `tape` is a symmetric soft clip (tanh-like); `tube` is asymmetric and adds even harmonics.
- Both use a drive derived from the amount, with gain compensation so loudness stays level.
- There is no oversampling. That was decided in `00-roadmap.md` (Round E, Skip), and the
  amount is capped so that aliasing stays low.

**Where it is stored and shown:**
- Stored per profile next to `preference`/`instruments`: `"dynamics": {"comp": "gentle",
  "color": {"kind": "tape", "amount": 0.3}}`.
- Presets carry it, `eq flat` drops it, and undo and history work through ConfigStore.
- `eq export` cannot express it for other tools: it warns and exports only the EQ part.
- `eq` shows a `dynamics:` line.
- `eq watch`: `c` cycles comp off→gentle→night, `v` cycles colour off→tape→tube, and `V`/`⇧v`
  changes the amount. Russian keys map to the same physical keys.
- `eq status --json` carries the compressor's current gain reduction, and the watch header
  shows it as `comp -3.2`.

**Tests:**
- Static curve at several levels: ratio, threshold and knee.
- Attack and release on a step are the mode's plus its RMS window, within 35 % and 20 %.
- Deep bass: a 40 Hz tone at −10 dBFS is compressed by its K-weighted level (night up to
  1.5 dB more, reading crests) and moves the gain by less than 0.15 dB over a cycle. This
  replaces the first version's test that it barely compressed at all.
- Balance (`CompressorBalanceTests`): gentle on the `favourite` curve keeps loudness within
  1 LU and octave bands within 1 dB of each other on music at −14 and −20 LUFS and on pink
  noise, leaves bass alone within 1 dB of the full mix, pumps less than 2.5 dB per beat, and
  still takes more than 2 dB off loud parts at −14 LUFS. Night brings explosions down by more
  than 1 dB against dialogue, lowers their peaks, and lifts quiet dialogue by more than 5 dB.
- Gentle's makeup survives a pause and the engine's reset after a second of silence.
- Colour: THD rises with the amount; `tube` has a 2nd harmonic and `tape` does not; the
  loudness difference stays within 1 dB.
- No NaN or denormals, and a golden impulse test with everything off stays bit-identical.
- Footprint: the CPU delta is measured once and written in the README.

## F2 — the curve follows the app that plays (experimental)

**Config**, `"apps"` at the top of `eq.json`, as an ordered list of rules:
`[{"app": "com.spotify.client", "preset": "favourite"}, {"app": "com.google.Chrome",
"preset": "voice"}]`. It is off unless `"experimental": {"apps": true}` is set.

**Detection:**
- Core Audio's process list (`kAudioHardwarePropertyProcessObjectList`) gives each
  process's `kAudioProcessPropertyBundleID` and `kAudioProcessPropertyIsRunningOutput`.
  These are public APIs.
- The daemon listens for property changes, with no polling if listeners are enough, and
  debounces for about 1 s.
- When several matching apps output at once, the tie-breaker is `nowplayingseek status
  --json` (the user's own tool, `/opt/homebrew/bin/nowplayingseek`), used only if it is
  installed and only on a tie. Otherwise the first rule in list order wins.

**Behaviour:**
- While a matching app plays, its preset is applied to the current device's curve as an
  overlay: the device profile's own preset/bands are the base and are restored when no
  matching app plays. The overlay is never written to eq.json, so undo and history are not
  polluted.
- `eq`, `eq watch` and `eq status` show `app: Spotify → favourite`.
- `eq events` gets an `app` event.

**CLI:**
- `eq app list`
- `eq app set <bundle-id|app name> <preset>`: the app name is resolved through Launch
  Services or the running processes.
- `eq app rm <app>`
- `eq app on|off`: toggles the experimental flag.
- Completions offer running audio apps and presets.

**Honest limit (README):** there is one curve for the whole system at a time. When two apps
play together, both use the winner's curve.

**Tests:**
- A fake process source drives the rule resolution: order, tie-break via an injected
  now-playing lookup, debounce, and restore when an app stops.
- The overlay never reaches eq.json.

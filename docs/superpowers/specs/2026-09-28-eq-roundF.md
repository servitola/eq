# eq Round F — dynamics and colour, a curve per app

Date: 2026-09-28. Status: approved in chat ("да, давай"). Per-app *mixing* (a separate tap and
curve for every app at once, as SoundSource does) is explicitly out of scope for now.

## F1 — light compression and colour

Both are small, cost nothing when off, and add tens of KB at most. The chain order is
EQ → compressor → colour → the existing limiter.

**Compressor** (`eq comp gentle|night|off [--device DEVICE]`):
- Feed-forward and stereo-linked. The detector signal goes through a high-pass filter at
  ~100 Hz, so bass does not pump the gain.
- Gain is smoothed in dB with attack and release coefficients and a soft knee.
- Makeup gain is automatic, so the modes change dynamics and not loudness.

| Mode | Ratio | Threshold | Knee | Attack | Release | Detector | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `gentle` | 2:1 | −18 dBFS | 6 dB | 30 ms | 250 ms | RMS | glue |
| `night` | 4:1 | −30 dBFS | 10 dB | 5 ms | 400 ms | RMS | levelling: quiet dialogue up, explosions down |

**Colour** (`eq color tape|tube <amount 0…1> | off [--device DEVICE]`):
- `tape` is a symmetric soft clip (tanh-like); `tube` is asymmetric and adds even harmonics.
- Both use a drive derived from the amount, with gain compensation so loudness stays level.
- There is no oversampling. That was decided in `00-roadmap.md` (Round E, Skip), and the
  amount is capped so that aliasing stays low.

**Where it is stored and shown:**
- Stored per profile next to `preference`/`instruments`: `"dynamics": {"comp": "gentle",
  "color": {"kind": "tape", "amount": 0.3}}`.
- Presets carry it, `eq flat` drops it, and undo and history work through ConfigStore.
- `eq export` cannot express it for other tools: it warns and exports only the EQ part.
- `eq` shows a `dynamics:` line.
- `eq watch`: `c` cycles comp off→gentle→night, `v` cycles colour off→tape→tube, and `V`/`⇧v`
  changes the amount. Russian keys map to the same physical keys.
- `eq status --json` carries the compressor's current gain reduction, and the watch header
  shows it as `comp -3.2`.

**Tests:**
- Static curve at several levels: ratio, threshold and knee.
- Attack and release times measured on a step input.
- The detector high-pass: a 40 Hz tone at −10 dBFS barely compresses, and the same level at
  1 kHz compresses.
- Colour: THD rises with the amount; `tube` has a 2nd harmonic and `tape` does not; the
  loudness difference stays within 1 dB.
- No NaN or denormals, and a golden impulse test with everything off stays bit-identical.
- Footprint: the CPU delta is measured once and written in the README.

## F2 — the curve follows the app that plays (experimental)

**Config**, `"apps"` at the top of `eq.json`, as an ordered list of rules:
`[{"app": "com.spotify.client", "preset": "favourite"}, {"app": "com.google.Chrome",
"preset": "voice"}]`. It is off unless `"experimental": {"apps": true}` is set.

**Detection:**
- Core Audio's process list (`kAudioHardwarePropertyProcessObjectList`) gives each
  process's `kAudioProcessPropertyBundleID` and `kAudioProcessPropertyIsRunningOutput`.
  These are public APIs.
- The daemon listens for property changes, with no polling if listeners are enough, and
  debounces for about 1 s.
- When several matching apps output at once, the tie-breaker is `nowplayingseek status
  --json` (the user's own tool, `/opt/homebrew/bin/nowplayingseek`), used only if it is
  installed and only on a tie. Otherwise the first rule in list order wins.

**Behaviour:**
- While a matching app plays, its preset is applied to the current device's curve as an
  overlay: the device profile's own preset/bands are the base and are restored when no
  matching app plays. The overlay is never written to eq.json, so undo and history are not
  polluted.
- `eq`, `eq watch` and `eq status` show `app: Spotify → favourite`.
- `eq events` gets an `app` event.

**CLI:**
- `eq app list`
- `eq app set <bundle-id|app name> <preset>`: the app name is resolved through Launch
  Services or the running processes.
- `eq app rm <app>`
- `eq app on|off`: toggles the experimental flag.
- Completions offer running audio apps and presets.

**Honest limit (README):** there is one curve for the whole system at a time. When two apps
play together, both use the winner's curve.

**Tests:**
- A fake process source drives the rule resolution: order, tie-break via an injected
  now-playing lookup, debounce, and restore when an app stops.
- The overlay never reaches eq.json.
